Prototype by Jake Schmidt · November 2026 How it works Source

What could knock Disney off course over the next decade, and what can it do about it today?

This tool plays out 1,000 possible futures for Disney against 109 competitors, economic shocks and new technologies. Then it shows which threats keep coming back and which low-cost actions blunt them.

How to use it

  1. Pick a strategyChoose one of Disney's six possible strategies in the menu below. Everything updates in about a second.
  2. Read the tabsStart with Threats & actions. Then compare strategies, or check the model against what really happened from 2019 to 2026.
  3. Change the assumptionsDisagree with a number? Open Adjust assumptions and move a slider, for one competitor or a whole industry.
