Let AI rivals play out the decade

Twelve AI agents play Disney, its biggest rivals and three newcomers for ten years. An AI referee scores every move, and an AI analyst tells you what Disney should do. Each run plays 3 games, each with a different cast of newcomers, and no two go the same way.

Leave it blank to simply play out the decade. If you fill it in, 4 games play in matched pairs, with and without your scenario, so you can see exactly what it changes.

    How the game works

    At the start of each game, a casting AI picks three newcomers to fit the moment and your scenario: one real company and two invented ones built on emerging technology. One player speaks for the ~100 smaller rivals the simulator tracks (regional parks, cruise lines, smaller studios), plus a light background drag of about 6% for everyday pressure. Each player is briefed on its company's real finances, assets and goals as of late 2026. Each turn covers two years: Disney moves, rivals react, and the referee judges what each move costs and how it changes Disney's profit. The strategy above and the shock and rival settings in Your assumptions apply. Rival players run on Claude Haiku 5.5, Disney and the referee on Claude Sonnet 5.5, and the analyst on Claude Opus 5.5. A run takes about 7–14 minutes.

        Starting point: fiscal 2026 segment operating income from Disney's FY26 Q4 8-K: Experiences $11.19B, Entertainment $5.44B, Sports $3.09B (total $19.7B). "Profit" on this page means total segment operating income.
