Prototype · strategic early warning system

Disney Strategy War Game

Simulates Disney against 109 companies across Experiences, Entertainment and Sports, plus 4 startup archetypes, thousands of times. It surfaces the threats that keep showing up in the bad futures, and backtests itself against FY19–FY26. Illustrative draft assumptions, not a forecast.

      Starting point: FY26 actuals from Disney's FY26 Q4 8-K: Experiences $11.19B, Entertainment $5.44B, Sports $3.09B segment operating income (total $19.7B).

      How it works: each simulated year, shocks and competitor moves fire with the probabilities set on the left. Each one cuts (or lifts) segment profit for a set number of years. The chosen playbook sets base growth and how exposed each segment is to each kind of threat. All impacts, probabilities and playbook settings are draft assumptions by Jake Schmidt, built to be overwritten with the team's own views and data.

      Backtest: starts from FY19 total segment OI of $14.85B (as recast in Disney's FY20 release). ESPN was not broken out in FY19, so the Sports starting value is an approximation. Threats no one could have listed in 2019, such as generative AI, are left out of the backtest deck.

      Not modeled yet: balance sheet limits, M&A by Disney, rebound dynamics after a shock, streaming margin as its own variable. Built as a prototype for discussion. Source and methodology on GitHub.
